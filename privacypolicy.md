Effective date: September 3, 2026 Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Studio Benwa ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications we publish on Google Play and the Apple App Store (each an "App", together the "Apps"). It applies to all Studio Benwa apps, including Word Search with Levels.

By downloading or using an App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Apps.

1. Summary
We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal contact information.
We do not operate servers that store your data. Your game progress is saved on your device.
Our Apps display advertising. Our third-party advertising partners automatically collect certain device and usage information in order to serve and measure ads. This is described in Section 3.
You can limit or opt out of personalized advertising at any time. See Section 6.
2. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit to ourselves any of the following:

Your name, email address, postal address, or phone number
Account credentials or user profiles (our Apps have no accounts and no sign-up)
Photos, contacts, calendar entries, microphone, or camera data
Precise GPS location
Payment card or financial account information
Our Apps work fully offline. Game progress, settings, statistics, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Advertising Partners
Our Apps are free and are supported by advertising. To serve ads, our advertising partners and their software development kits (SDKs) operate inside the Apps and may automatically collect and process the following:

Device advertising identifier — the Google Advertising ID (Android) or the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA (iOS)
IP address, and an approximate location (such as country, region, or city) derived from it
Device and technical information — device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type, and mobile carrier
App and ad interaction data — which ads were requested, shown, viewed, clicked, or closed, and general in-app events used to measure ad performance
Crash and diagnostic information related to ad delivery
This information is collected by the advertising partners directly. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying performance reports (for example, total impressions, clicks, and revenue). We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address, and we cannot identify you personally from these reports.

Depending on your consent and settings, this information may be used for personalized (interest-based) advertising, which can involve tracking across apps and websites. Where you do not consent, or where you have opted out, our partners serve non-personalized ads, which still require limited data such as coarse location and basic device information for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad delivery.

5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)
On iOS devices, our Apps use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be shown a system prompt asking whether you allow the App to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

If you allow tracking, your device's IDFA may be used by our advertising partners for personalized advertising.
If you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not made available and you will receive non-personalized ads.
You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

6. Your Choices and Controls
Android — You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device and Android version).

iOS — You can withdraw tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and limit ad tracking system-wide in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Google ad personalization — You can manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com

Uninstalling — Uninstalling an App removes all locally stored game data from your device.

7. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland
If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, our Apps present a consent message the first time you launch them, delivered through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform. You may choose whether to consent to personalized advertising and to the associated processing by our advertising partners.

The legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
The legal basis for limited processing necessary to deliver non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and secure the service is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
You may withdraw or change your consent at any time from the privacy or settings screen inside the App.

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Because we do not hold identifiable data about you ourselves, requests concerning data collected by our advertising partners should be directed to those partners using the links in Section 4. We are happy to assist — contact us at the address in Section 12.

8. Users in California and Other U.S. States
We do not sell personal information for money. However, the use of advertising identifiers for personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "share" for cross-context behavioral advertising under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and comparable state laws.

You may opt out of this by disabling ad personalization or resetting your advertising identifier as described in Section 6, and by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS.

California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Requests may be sent to the contact address in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy
Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our Apps, please contact us at the address in Section 12 and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Data Retention and Security
We do not operate servers and do not retain personal data about you. Game data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Our advertising partners retain data in accordance with their own retention policies, which are described in the privacy policies linked in Section 4.

We take reasonable measures to protect our Apps, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
